Abandoned but not forgotten

POSTCARDS FROM THE SOLAR SYSTEM

Since the 1960s, NASA has left robots behind on the Moon, on Mars, and out in deep space. Their missions are over, but they still have stories to tell. Each one has written postcards to send back to Earth.

  1. 1Pick a world. Click the Moon, Mars, Ceres, or Eros to get a postcard from one of the machines left there.
  2. 2Read it. Each postcard is written by the machine itself. Press Done reading to go back to space.
  3. 3Look into it. Use the buttons under a postcard to open real NASA pages, photos, and data.
  4. 4Collect them all. A tracker at the bottom shows which machines you have heard from. Fill all the dots to collect every postcard, and click a name to read one again.
